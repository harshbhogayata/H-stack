# Grown-up areas and privacy

- Settings, goals, sign-in and anything that leaves the app (sharing, export, links) open only through `<ParentGate>`. The gate asks a grown-up question (e.g. "Type the number seventeen in digits") and never relies on age self-declaration.
- Readers' progress is stored on the device (`src/data/progress.js`). Nothing is sent to our servers except an optional backup a grown-up turns on.
- No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking of readers. We follow COPPA and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code: data minimisation, high privacy by default.
- A reader's display name is a first name or nickname only; we never ask readers for an email.
