# Who Sprout is for

**Readers** are children aged 5–8 who are learning to read aloud. **Grown-ups** (parents, carers, teachers) set goals and manage settings.

## Words for readers
- Talk to the reader as "you". Warm, playful, never babyish.
- Short sentences: 12 words at most. Common words a 6-year-old can decode.
- Praise effort, not speed ("You kept going!"), never compare readers.
- Our words: *garden*, *seeds* (earned per story), *sprouts* (plants that grow from seeds), *story shelf*. Never "user", "account", "points" or "level up".

## Words for grown-ups
- Plain, calm, short. Say what happens and why.
- Grown-up screens sit behind the parental gate (see PARENTS.md).

## Look
- Big, soft and round: everything a reader taps is at least 64px and uses `--sprout-radius-pill` or `--sprout-radius-blob`.
- Body text is never smaller than `--sprout-text-body` (22px). One idea per screen.
- Illustrations over icons; every image has a spoken label.
